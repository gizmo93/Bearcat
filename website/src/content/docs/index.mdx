## What is Bearcat?

Bearcat runs on your desktop, NAS, or server. Use its web interface to manage releases,
create archives, and upload them to your hosters. Bearcat checks the links and reuploads offline files.

You can:

- [Automatically download releases from FTP/FTPS servers](/remote-downloads/), verify SFV checksums, and optionally extract RAR or 7z archives.
- Pack releases into RAR or 7z archives, or upload archives you already have.
- Upload to Rapidgator, DDownload, Keep2Share, and the other hosters listed below.
- Free local disk space and [restore archives from a mirror hoster](/mirror-downloads/) when a reupload needs them.
- Look up release information and NFOs through xREL and SRRDB, movie or TV metadata through TMDB and TheTVDB, and game metadata through Steam.
- Read resolution, codecs, and audio and subtitle tracks from your video files with MediaInfo.
- Upload cover images and [create video screenshots or screenshot grids](/release-templates-and-automations/#screenshots). Use the images alongside download links and NFOs in [post templates](/forum-post-templates/).
- [Prepare drafts on forums and WordPress blogs](/posting-to-forums/) and submit them yourself, or use [posting rules to post automatically](/automatic-forum-posting/).
- Find releases ready to post in the [post queue](/post-queue/).

## Common questions

### Can I use PostgreSQL?

Yes. New installations use SQLite, but you can [set up PostgreSQL](/install-postgresql-for-desktop/) or use the [Docker option](/use-the-docker-image/#database). Existing PostgreSQL installations keep their settings.

### Can I upload existing archives?

Yes. Use an unmanaged release for existing archives, or a managed release to let Bearcat create them.
See [release types](/release-types/).

### Do I have to keep every archive on disk?

No. Enable a hoster as a mirror so Bearcat can download archives when needed for a reupload.
Delete local copies yourself or set a retention period for automatic cleanup.

The hoster must support downloads through its API, and some hosters require a premium account.
See [mirror downloads](/mirror-downloads/) for supported hosters and setup instructions.
